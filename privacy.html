Privacy Policy

Sunlight · Last updated October 20, 2026

Sunlight does not collect, use, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

Your game data

Your scores, stats, streaks, Sunbeams, Shop items and settings are saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also kept in your own private iCloud account so they follow you to your other devices and a new phone. This is handled by Apple, and we can't see it. Your game data is never sent to us. You can turn off iCloud for the app in the Settings app at any time.

Weather and location

The game looks up the current weather for the cities in each round using Apple Weather. Weather results are not stored.

If you allow it, Sunlight uses your approximate location to show the weather where you are on the home screen. Your location is only sent to Apple Weather to look up that weather. It is never stored, never sent to us and never shared with anyone else. You can say no and still play everything, or turn location off at any time in the Settings app.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can play without Game Center.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
