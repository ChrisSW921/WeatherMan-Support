Common questions

How do I play?
Each round shows a city. Pick the temperature range you think it is right now, choose how many tokens to stake, and lock in. Some rounds ask which of two cities is warmer or colder. Settings has the full rules.

How do I earn Sunbeams?
Make it through at least 3 rounds of a run with tokens left. Daily Forecasts, streaks and milestones earn extra.

The weather didn't load.
Live weather needs an internet connection. Check your connection and start a new run.

My scores aren't on the leaderboard.
Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the Settings app on your device.

Does it cost anything?
No. There are no ads and nothing to buy with real money. Tokens and Sunbeams are only earned by playing.
